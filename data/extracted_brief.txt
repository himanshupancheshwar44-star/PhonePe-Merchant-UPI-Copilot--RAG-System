UPI Payment Problems Faced by Small Merchants in India

Research brief and starter knowledge base for a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) application

1. Project opportunity

Build a “Merchant UPI Helpdesk” that helps hotel/restaurant operators, shopkeepers, and small vendors understand what to do when a UPI payment is pending, a customer shows a success screen but the merchant has not received the money, a refund is delayed, or a suspicious payment claim appears. The system should retrieve trusted guidance and explain next steps with source citations—not guess whether a particular payment succeeded.

2. Real issues documented in public sources

Payment failure or service outage

UPI or a payment app may be temporarily unavailable, leaving customers unable to pay and merchants unable to complete sales. A news report about the April 2025 outage described disruption to retail and wholesale markets in Uttar Pradesh.

Possible merchant impact: queues, interrupted service, lost or postponed sales.

RAG question: “UPI is not working at my shop right now. What should I tell customers and what should I check?”

Source: Times of India, 12–13 Apr 2025

Payment shows pending / confirmation is delayed

NPCI’s UPI FAQ explains that a transaction can show pending when the payer is debited but confirmation/credit at the beneficiary bank is delayed.

Possible merchant impact: uncertainty about whether to hand over goods, repeat-payment confusion, and time spent checking status.

RAG question: “Customer says money was deducted, but my account has not received it. What is the safe next step?”

Source: NPCI UPI FAQ

Payer debited, merchant not credited; reversal or refund delay

NPCI says failed transactions where the payer was debited are expected to be refunded, but sometimes the refund takes longer; its FAQ advises contacting the bank if the refund has not arrived within the stated timeframe.

Possible merchant impact: disputes at the counter and repeated customer follow-ups.

RAG question: “The customer was debited and the transaction failed. Who should raise a complaint, and where?”

Source: NPCI UPI FAQ

Merchant cannot reconcile payments quickly

NPCI says merchants can raise grievances or check transaction status through their acquiring bank. This indicates that merchant-side status checking and escalation are important workflows.

Possible merchant impact: manual checking of bank/app records and difficulty closing daily accounts.

RAG question: “How can a merchant check a disputed transaction and escalate it?”

Source: NPCI UPI FAQ

Fraud and misleading payment claims

A 2025 merchant report identifies scam protection and low awareness of redress processes as concerns for some merchants. A customer showing a screenshot or notification is not the same as the merchant independently confirming credit.

Possible merchant impact: goods handed over before funds are confirmed, loss, or uncertainty about reporting a suspicious incident.

RAG question: “A customer shows a payment screenshot, but I cannot see the credit. What should I verify?”

Source: Understanding UPI Report, 2025

Poor connectivity and inconsistent notifications

The 2025 merchant report and news coverage describe connectivity problems, downtime, and delayed notifications as pain points affecting merchants.

Possible merchant impact: longer queues, repeated payment attempts, and customer frustration.

RAG question: “My internet is weak and payment confirmation is delayed. What should I do without charging the customer twice?”

Source: Understanding UPI Report, 2025; Times of India, Apr 2025

Operational disruption in hotels and restaurants

A report on the April 2025 disruption quoted a restaurant operator describing customers unable to pay during the outage and staff noting contact details for later payment.

Possible merchant impact: table turnover slows, bills remain unsettled, and staff must track later payments.

RAG question: “During a UPI outage, how can a restaurant record unpaid bills safely and follow up?”

Source: Times of India, 12 Apr 2025

Disputes and chargeback handling are complicated

An NPCI circular from February 2025 discusses chargeback handling and coordination between remitting and beneficiary banks for certain transaction cases.

Possible merchant impact: confusing escalation steps and uncertainty about who must act.

RAG question: “A UPI transaction is disputed. What evidence should I keep and which bank channel should I contact?”

Source: NPCI circular, 10 Feb 2025

3. Evidence quality: what is known vs. what to validate

The issues above are grounded in public NPCI guidance, an NPCI circular, and published reporting. They establish that these problems occur, but they do not establish how often each issue affects every hotel or shopkeeper. Treat merchant impact statements as plausible consequences unless the cited source directly reports them. Before building a commercial product, interview local merchants and record their own experiences—with consent—rather than presenting invented stories or synthetic examples as real testimony.

4. Recommended RAG use case

Product name (working): Merchant UPI Helpdesk

Input: merchant describes the issue, approximate time, amount range, app/bank involved if they choose to share it, and the visible transaction status.

Retrieve: official NPCI FAQs, the merchant’s acquiring bank help pages, relevant RBI directions, and carefully selected incident reports.

Answer: give a short checklist, say what is known and unknown, and cite the exact source and section.

Escalate: route unresolved cases to the merchant’s acquiring bank/payment provider. Do not claim to check live payment status unless an authorized payment-system integration actually exists.

Safety: never ask users to share UPI PINs, OTPs, full account numbers, or other secrets. Do not advise handing over goods based only on a screenshot; advise checking the merchant’s own bank/app record.

Evaluation: test whether the answer cites the right policy, avoids inventing refund times, distinguishes payer and merchant roles, and knows when to say “contact your bank”.

6. Questions to ask shopkeepers and hotel operators

What is the most common UPI issue you face during a normal week?

What do you do when the customer says “payment successful” but you do not see the credit?

How do you check a payment later, and how long does that usually take in your experience?

Have failed or delayed payments ever interrupted a busy period? What happened?

Which apps, bank accounts, QR devices, or soundboxes do you use?

How do you record a bill when payment is pending?

Have you encountered fake screenshots, misleading notifications, or suspicious requests? What support did you receive?

What would you want an AI assistant to answer, and what would you never trust it to decide?
